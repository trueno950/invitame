Risograph, three inks

What makes it read as hand-made
Three inks only — the theme's green, its orange and the near-black — as flat fills, plus two SVG dot screens set at 18° and ‑24°. Every plate is off register by 1.5-2.5 px, so colour fringes and half-covered dots show; the printed sheet itself lies 1.3° off square; a 14 % grain and loose ink specks imitate the duplicator drum. No gradients, no shadows, no soft edges anywhere.
Cost for 28 themes
The cheapest and the most systematic: one silhouette, two screens, two offsets. 45-60 paths and 2-3 h per theme, mostly choosing which three inks carry the theme; the screens are shared by all 28. Scene SVG 15.8 kB, but the patterns and the filter are most of those bytes, not drawing.
Palette, font, assets
Seven colours available, three used — that discipline is the style. Themes with many hues (under the sea, princess) lose detail when reduced to three inks. Lilita One is the product font; the stack here falls back to «Arial Rounded MT Bold». No external images, no motion.
