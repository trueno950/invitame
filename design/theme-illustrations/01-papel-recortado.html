Paper cut-out

What makes it read as hand-made
Every element is a separate cut sheet with its own 3-4 px offset shadow, never a symmetric glow. The top edge is torn: an irregular line with notches of different depth, not a zigzag. The hills, ground and fronds come from hand-placed splines, not arcs; the plates come in four sizes and two tilts; the tail and the crest do not close exactly on the body; the footprints sit at four different angles. A feTurbulence grain at 13 % is multiplied over the scene, so nothing stays flat vector.
Cost for 28 themes
One base scene sheet, one character sheet (a 38-point silhouette plus details), 3-5 props, a divider and a footer strip: about 5-8 h of real drawing per theme. Scene SVG 11.0 kB, whole illustration set 17.6 kB; almost all of it is path data, so the frames stay cheap to ship inline.
Palette, font, assets
The theme's seven colours only, tints by opacity. Display font is Lilita One in the product (via next/font); this artboard shows the system stack «Arial Rounded MT Bold». No external images, no motion, one filter for grain.
