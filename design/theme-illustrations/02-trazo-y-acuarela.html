Ink line and wash

What makes it read as hand-made
The outline is not one vector contour: it is three overlapping ink strokes of different weight (3.1 / 2.6 / 1.9) whose ends overshoot the join, over a point-to-point 1 px wobble. The washes are painted above and below the line, offset 3-4 px and multiplied, so they never meet the contour — the way water ignores a pen. The grass wash is laid in front of the feet and the pigment darkens under the belly. feTurbulence paper grain at 9 %, ink-dab doodles, a spiral that stops where the pen lifted.
Cost for 28 themes
The most expensive: a filled path cannot be reused as a line, so every contour is redrawn piece by piece — about 55-75 strokes, 5-8 washes and 10 doodles per theme, 6-8 h per theme. The 1 px wobble can be scripted from a hand-placed point list, and that is exactly where it stops feeling drawn.
Palette, font, assets
The theme's seven colours, with multiply so two washes make a third tone. Lilita One is the product font; the stack here falls back to «Arial Rounded MT Bold». No external images, no motion, no shadows.
